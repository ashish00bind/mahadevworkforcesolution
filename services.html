Workflow Methodology

Our 4-Stage Deployment Process

How we intake your facility requirements and mobilize productive, compliant teams without operational delays.

01

Requirement Assessment

We consult with your plant or HR team to understand headcount, shift hours, specific job descriptions, skill levels, and statutory expectations.

02

Sourcing & Role Matching

Our recruitment network sources candidates matching trade specifications, experience criteria, and proximity to your facility.

03

Screening & Document Checks

Every candidate undergoes KYC verification, police record checks, statutory document collection (Aadhaar, Bank, PF UAN), and health verification.

04

Deployment & Onboarding

Workers are deployed on-site with complete PPE kits, briefed on plant rules and safety instructions under the guidance of our supervisor.
